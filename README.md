# FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard 📊

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard** developed to analyze sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product and category contribution, geographical demand, seasonal trends, and delivery efficiency.

The objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decisions related to **inventory planning, marketing campaigns, product promotion, regional targeting, and delivery optimization**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze FNP sales data and answer important business questions such as:

- What is the overall sales and order performance?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which locations generate the highest revenue?
- Which months have the highest and lowest revenue?
- Which products contribute the most revenue?
- Which cities have the highest number of orders?
- During which hours do customers generate the most revenue?
- How long does it take to deliver an order?
- How does sales performance vary across different occasions?

# 🔎 Key Business Insights

## 1. Revenue Performance

The business generated approximately **₹35.21 lakh from 1,000 orders**, with an average customer spend of approximately **₹3,521**.

This provides a high-level view of the overall sales performance.

---

## 2. Category Performance

**Colors** is the highest-revenue category, followed by **Soft Toys** and **Sweets**.

These categories are important contributors to overall revenue and could receive additional attention in areas such as inventory planning, promotions, and product bundling.

---

## 3. Seasonal Sales Trends

Revenue varies significantly across different months.

The dashboard shows notable peaks around **February, August, and November**, indicating that sales are influenced by seasonal and occasion-based demand.

This highlights the importance of preparing inventory and marketing campaigns ahead of high-demand periods.

---

## 4. Product Performance

The Top 5 Products analysis highlights products such as **Magma Set, Quia Gift, Delores Gift, Harum Pack, and Deserunt Box** as important revenue contributors.

These products can be further investigated to understand what drives their performance.

---

## 5. Geographic Performance

Revenue and order volume vary considerably across different locations and cities.

Cities with higher order volumes can be analyzed further to understand:

- Customer preferences
- Average order value
- Popular product categories
- Occasion-based demand
- Regional purchasing patterns

---

## 6. Ordering Time

Revenue shows noticeable activity during the evening period, particularly around **7 PM–8 PM**.

This could be useful when planning time-specific marketing campaigns, promotions, or customer engagement activities.

---

## 7. Delivery Performance

The average order-to-delivery time is **5.53 days**.

Further analysis by city, category, occasion, and month could help identify locations or periods where delivery times are higher.

# 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

### 1. Focus on High-Performing Categories

Maintain sufficient inventory and promotional support for major revenue-generating categories such as **Colors, Soft Toys, and Sweets**.

### 2. Plan for Seasonal Demand

Use historical sales trends to forecast demand and prepare inventory before high-sales periods.

### 3. Develop Location-Specific Strategies

Analyze high-revenue and high-order-volume locations separately and develop targeted marketing strategies for different regions.

### 4. Promote High-Performing Products

Use top-performing products in:

- Product bundles
- Occasion-based collections
- Cross-selling
- Personalized recommendations
- Promotional campaigns

### 5. Explore Evening Sales Opportunities

Since revenue activity is noticeable during evening hours, the business can test targeted promotions and customer engagement campaigns during these periods.

### 6. Improve Delivery Analysis

Analyze delivery performance by:

- City
- Product category
- Occasion
- Month
- Order volume

This can help identify potential delivery bottlenecks.

### 7. Include Profitability Analysis

Revenue alone does not provide the complete picture of business performance.

Future analysis should include:

- Profit
- Profit margin
- Discounts
- Shipping costs
- Returns
- Customer acquisition cost

This would help identify which products and locations are not only generating revenue but also contributing to profitability.
